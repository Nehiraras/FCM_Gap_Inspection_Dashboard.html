Flagged units proceed to OMM for dimensional confirmation. D191 shows a consistently low CCD rate (~0.04%). D192 shows elevated flags on some days (peak 0.52% on 8/22), with only 5 OMM-confirmed true defects across the full run — confirming a very low true defect rate (<0.001%).
